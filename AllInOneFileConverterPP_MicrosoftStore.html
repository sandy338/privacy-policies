Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Techflyer ("we", "our", or "the Company") handles your information in connection with the All In One File Converter application for Windows ("the Application").

Privacy Commitment: We value your privacy. Our primary goal is to provide file conversion services without compromising your personal data or document security.

1. Data Processing (How Conversions Work)

Depending on the technical requirements of the conversion, the Application processes data in the following way:

  • Local Conversion: Where possible, the Application performs conversions directly on your local device. In these cases, your files never leave your computer.
  • No Permanent Storage: We do not store your original files or the converted output on any permanent servers. Once the conversion process is complete and the file is saved to your chosen destination, the temporary data used for the conversion is cleared from the Application's memory.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The Application is designed to function without requiring user registration or accounts. We do not collect:

  • Personally Identifiable Information (Name, Email, Phone number).
  • Your file contents or metadata for any purpose other than the conversion you requested.
  • Browsing history or data from other applications on your device.

3. Windows Permissions

To provide its services, the Application may request the following permissions on Windows:

  • File System Access: Required to read the file you wish to convert and to save the converted file to your selected folder.
  • Broad File Access (if applicable): Used solely to allow you to browse for files across your local drives within the app interface.

4. Microsoft Store Telemetry

As a Microsoft Store app, standard anonymous technical data may be collected by Microsoft and shared with us in an aggregated format. This includes:

  • Crash reports and error logs to help us improve app stability.
  • Usage statistics (e.g., number of downloads, regional distribution).

This data is managed by Microsoft in accordance with their privacy standards and does not identify you personally.

5. Third-Party Services

The Application does not contain third-party advertising SDKs or trackers. If any specific conversion engine requires an external API, that data is transmitted securely (via HTTPS) and is used exclusively for the conversion requested by the user.

6. Security

We take the security of your data seriously. Because the app functions primarily locally, your data's security is also protected by your Windows operating system's built-in security features and your personal device security settings.

7. Children's Privacy

The Application is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect data from children. It is safe for use by individuals of all ages.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes in our app's features or legal requirements. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

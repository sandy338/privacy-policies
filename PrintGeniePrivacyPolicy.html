Privacy Policy for Printer Genie

Last Updated: October 26, 2023

1. Introduction

Printer Genie (“we,” “our,” or “the Application”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Printer Genie Windows desktop application.

By installing or using Printer Genie, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies solely to the Printer Genie desktop application and does not apply to third-party websites accessed through the Application.

3. Information We Handle

Printer Genie is designed with a privacy-first, local-processing approach.

3.1 Information Processed Locally

The following information is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers:

  • Printer discovery and device details
  • Hardware and system information: This includes your operating system version and architecture (e.g., Windows 11 64-bit), used locally to help find compatible drivers.
  • Print queue data
  • PDF files selected for printing
  • Application preferences and settings
  • Diagnostic operations: The application may run checks on your system's printer configuration and present a summary of the results directly to you within the app. This information is for your use only and is not sent to us.

3.2 Stepper Questionnaire Data

Printer Genie includes a guided stepper interface where users manually select their printer brand, model, and other details. This information is used solely to request the correct driver URLs from our server and is not stored or used for profiling.

3.3 API Communication

Printer Genie communicates with our API for essential functions. When it does, we send the minimum information necessary:

a) Driver URL Retrieval: The questionnaire selections (brand, model, etc.) are sent to our API to retrieve relevant manufacturer driver links.

b) Auto-Detected Printer Lookup: If you enable this feature, we send only the non-unique manufacturer and model name provided by your device (e.g., "HP LaserJet Pro M404n") to check for driver availability. No unique hardware IDs, serial numbers, or network identifiers are transmitted.

4. What We Do NOT Collect

Printer Genie is explicitly designed not to collect or store:

  • Personal identity information (names, emails, phone numbers)
  • The contents of your documents or PDF files
  • Your browsing activity or history
  • Persistent usage tracking data or profiles of your activity

5. Third-Party Manufacturer Websites

Printer Genie may open official manufacturer websites inside an in-app dialog. We do not control these third-party websites, do not collect data from those sessions, and are not responsible for their privacy practices. Users should review the manufacturer’s privacy policy.

6. How Information Is Used

Information handled by Printer Genie is used exclusively for:

  • Printer detection and driver link matching
  • Core printing functionality and compatibility checks
  • Presenting diagnostic summaries to you within the app
  • Facilitating communication with our API for application functionality

No data is used for advertising, marketing, or resale.

7. Data Storage

All operational data related to your files and printers is stored locally on your device. We do not maintain remote databases of user activity or print history.

8. Data Security

Printer Genie relies on the security mechanisms built into the Windows operating system. While reasonable safeguards are applied to our API, no system is completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Because most data is processed locally, it remains on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the Application removes its associated local data. Any temporary data related to API requests is not retained by us.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data with third parties. Information may only be disclosed if required by applicable law or a binding legal obligation.

11. User Control

Users may enable or disable printer auto-detection, manually select all printer details, and decline to use any optional features.

12. Children’s Privacy

Printer Genie is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

13. International Use

By using the Application, you acknowledge and consent that any data transmitted during API communication may be transferred to and processed on servers that may be located outside of your home country.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Your continued use of Printer Genie after an update constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

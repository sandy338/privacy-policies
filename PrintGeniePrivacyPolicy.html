Privacy Policy for Printer Genie

Last Updated: [Insert Date]

1. Introduction

Printer Genie (“we,” “our,” or “the Application”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Printer Genie Windows desktop application.

By installing or using Printer Genie, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies solely to the Printer Genie desktop application and does not apply to third-party websites accessed through the Application.

3. Information We Handle

Printer Genie is designed with a privacy-first, local-processing approach.

3.1 Information Processed Locally

The following information is processed locally on your device:

  • Printer discovery and device details
  • Hardware and system information
  • Print queue data
  • PDF files selected for printing
  • Application preferences and settings
  • Diagnostic operations

This information does not leave your computer unless explicitly stated below.

3.2 Stepper Questionnaire Data

Printer Genie includes a guided stepper interface where users manually select:

  • Printer brand
  • Region
  • Printer model
  • Operating system (auto-detected but user selectable)

Important:

  • Questionnaire selections are not stored permanently by us.
  • Responses are not transmitted for tracking or profiling.
  • Responses are used solely to request driver URLs.

3.3 API Communication

Printer Genie communicates with an API only for functional purposes:

a) Driver URL Retrieval

Selected questionnaire options may be sent to retrieve:

  • Relevant manufacturer driver links
  • Compatibility results

b) Auto-Detected Printer Lookup

If printer auto-detection is enabled:

  • Printer model
  • Basic device identifiers

may be sent to check for known driver availability.

4. What We Do NOT Collect

Printer Genie does not collect or store:

  • Personal identity information
  • Names, emails, phone numbers
  • Documents or PDF contents
  • Browsing activity
  • Questionnaire history
  • Persistent usage tracking data

We do not build user profiles.

5. Third-Party Manufacturer Websites

Printer Genie may open official manufacturer websites inside an in-app dialog/browser.

We:

  • Do not control those websites
  • Do not collect data from those sessions
  • Are not responsible for their privacy practices

Users should review the manufacturer’s privacy policy when visiting external sites.

6. How Information Is Used

Information handled by Printer Genie is used exclusively for:

  • Printer detection
  • Driver link matching
  • Compatibility checks
  • Printing functionality
  • Diagnostics
  • Application stability

No data is used for advertising or resale.

7. Data Storage

  • All operational data is stored locally on your device.
  • We do not maintain cloud databases of user activity.
  • No remote storage of documents or printer usage.

8. Data Security

Printer Genie relies on:

  • Windows security mechanisms
  • Local system protections

While reasonable safeguards are applied, no system is completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Because data is processed locally:

  • Data remains until deleted by the user.
  • Uninstalling the Application removes associated local data.
  • We do not retain remote copies.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade data.

Information may only be disclosed if required by:

  • Applicable law
  • Legal obligations

11. User Control

Users may:

  • Enable/disable printer auto-detection
  • Choose questionnaire selections manually
  • Decline optional features

12. Children’s Privacy

Printer Genie is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

13. International Use

Printer Genie may be used globally. Any API communication may involve servers located in different jurisdictions.

By using the Application, you consent to such transfers where necessary for functionality.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically.

Updates may occur due to:

  • Feature changes
  • Legal requirements

Continued use of Printer Genie constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Privacy Policy for ScreenCapture

Effective Date: September 9, 2026

At Techflyer, we believe that your screen content is private. ScreenCapture is designed as a local-first utility, ensuring that your screenshots and recordings remain under your control.

1. Data Processing and Local Storage

ScreenCapture processes all data locally on your device. We do not transmit your screen content, audio recordings, or metadata to any external servers.

  • Local Storage: Screenshots and screen recordings are saved directly to your local device. This ensures you do not lose your captures when the application is closed and allows you to access or manage your files at any time.
  • No Cloud Uploads: The application does not automatically upload, backup, or share your media files with any third-party services.

2. Audio and Media Access

  • Audio: The application only accesses your audio input device if you explicitly enable audio during a screen recording session. We do not monitor or record audio in the background without your active consent.
  • Control: You have full control over the storage location of your captured media files.

3. Background Operation and Startup

ScreenCapture functions as a background utility to ensure that keyboard shortcuts remain responsive.

  • Background Service: Running in the background allows the application to listen for your configured hotkeys, providing instant access to capture tools without requiring you to manually open the interface.
  • Startup Launch: The application may register to launch at Windows startup solely to provide immediate access to these keyboard shortcuts. This does not involve automatic recording or background data collection.

4. Offline Operation

ScreenCapture is designed for total offline functionality. All core features—including capturing, recording, and managing files—work without an internet connection. This design choice minimizes the risk of sensitive screen content being transmitted inadvertently.

5. User-Initiated Sharing

The application does not share your data. Any action to "Share," "Copy," or "Open" a file is initiated exclusively by you. The application acts only as a tool for you to manage your own files on your own device.

6. Data Retention

We do not manage or delete your files on your behalf. Captured media files remain on your device until you manually choose to move, copy, or delete them. We do not have access to these files, and we cannot recover them if they are deleted from your local storage.

7. Third-Party Services

ScreenCapture does not integrate with tracking SDKs or third-party advertising services. Your privacy is maintained through our focus on local, offline software architecture.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our functionality. Any updates will be posted to this page.

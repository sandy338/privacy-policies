All In One File Reader is designed to operate as a local utility. When you use the Application to open, view, or access documents, spreadsheets, text files, images, or videos, all processing occurs entirely on your local device.

  • No File Uploads: Your files (including PDFs, CSVs, Excel files, images, and videos) are not uploaded to our servers, nor are they shared with any third parties.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Techflyer ("we", "our") manages your data within the All In One File Reader application for macOS. We are committed to a "Privacy-First" approach, ensuring your files never leave your Mac.

Privacy Summary: We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal documents. All file processing happens locally on your machine.

1. Local-Only Processing & Sandboxing

The Application is built using Apple’s recommended security practices for macOS:

  • App Sandboxing: The Application operates within a secure "sandbox" environment. This limits the app's access to only the files you explicitly choose to open.
  • No Cloud Uploads: When you open a PDF, Excel sheet, image, or video, the data is read directly from your local disk into your Mac's memory. No data is sent to our servers or any third-party cloud.
  • No Data Retention: We do not keep a history of your files or cache your sensitive documents after the Application is closed.

2. macOS System Permissions

To function correctly on macOS, the Application may request the following permissions:

  • Files and Folders: Required to read and display the contents of the files you select.
  • Downloads Folder: May be requested if you choose to open files directly from your system's Downloads directory.

You can manage or revoke these permissions at any time via System Settings > Privacy & Security > Files and Folders.

3. Data Collection & Analytics

We do not collect personal identifiers (such as your name, email, or IP address). However, through the Mac App Store, Apple may provide us with:

  • Diagnostic Data: If you have opted-in to share "Share with App Developers" in your macOS settings, we may receive anonymous crash logs to help us fix bugs.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous metrics (e.g., how many times the app was downloaded) provided by Apple’s App Analytics. This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you.

4. In-App Purchases (If Applicable)

If the Application offers a Pro version or features via In-App Purchase, all transactions are handled exclusively by Apple via the Mac App Store. We do not have access to your credit card information or billing address.

5. Third-Party Links

The Application may contain a link to our support website. Clicking these links will open your default web browser (e.g., Safari). These external sites have their own privacy policies which we encourage you to review.

6. Children’s Privacy

This Application does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13. It is a utility tool designed for a general audience.

7. Compliance with macOS Standards

This policy is designed to comply with Apple’s App Store Review Guidelines regarding User Privacy and Data Use. We do not sell user data to third parties, and we do not use tracking software (SDKs) for advertising.

Photo Library Access

To perform its core function, MediaSweep requires your permission to access your device’s Photo Library. This permission is essential for the Application to identify duplicate images and videos stored locally on your device.

  • Purpose of Access: Access is used solely to scan your media files on-device to find duplicates.
  • Data Privacy: The Application does not upload, share, or transfer your photos or videos to any external servers. All scanning and analysis occur locally on your device.
  • Permission Control: You can manage the Application’s access to your Photo Library at any time in your device's Settings.

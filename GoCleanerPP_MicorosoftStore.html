Privacy Policy - System Cleaner for Windows

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Privacy Commitment: System Cleaner for Windows is an offline-first utility. Your data, browsing history, and file contents never leave your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal information.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. The application is designed to function entirely offline. It does not require an account, login, or internet connection to perform its core functions.

2. How We Handle Your Files

The application scans your device to identify junk files (such as temporary files, browser caches, and crash dumps) to help you free up disk space. Our handling of your data follows these strict rules:

  • No Content Inspection: The application does not read or analyze the actual content of your personal documents. It only identifies files by metadata (file paths and sizes) to determine if they are junk.
  • User-Controlled Deletion: No files are deleted automatically. All deletions are initiated and confirmed by you via the "Clean Files" action.
  • Files in Use: The application respects active system processes. Files currently in use by other applications or the Windows OS are automatically skipped to ensure system stability.

3. Browser Data

If you choose to use the "Deep Clean" feature to clear browser caches, cookies, or history, the application interacts with your local browser data stores. This process happens locally on your computer. We do not have access to your browser history, and we do not store or transmit this information.

Note: Deleting cookies will log you out of websites in your browser, which is a standard result of clearing browser cache.

4. Offline Operation

As an offline utility, the application does not have the capability to connect to the internet. Your system data remains local to your device at all times.

5. Security and Safety

  • No Registry Changes: The application does not modify the Windows Registry.
  • Safe Cleanup: We only remove temporary files and logs. We do not touch your personal documents, photos, or system-critical files.
  • No Administrative Escalation: The application runs with standard user privileges and does not require administrative rights to operate.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect future feature additions. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
